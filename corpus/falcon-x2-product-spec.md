# Falcon X2 Spraying Drone — Product Specification


## Overview

Falcon X2 is Lumen's second-generation agricultural spraying drone, designed for row crops, orchards, and vineyards. It launched in March 2026 and replaces the Falcon X1.

## Flight and payload

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Take-off weight (empty, with battery) | 24 kg |
| Maximum payload | 10 kg |
| Flight time, empty | 42 minutes |
| Flight time, full payload | 28 minutes |
| Maximum wind resistance | 12 m/s |
| Operating temperature | -10 °C to 45 °C |
| Ingress protection | IP67 |

## Spraying system

The spray system covers a swath up to 7 metres wide. In typical field conditions Falcon X2 treats up to 22 hectares per hour. The tank holds 10 litres, and nozzle flow is adjustable from 0.5 to 4 litres per minute.

## Navigation and safety

- RTK GPS positioning with ±2 cm horizontal accuracy.
- Forward and rear radar obstacle avoidance with a 40-metre detection range.
- Automatic return-to-home when the battery falls below 20% or the control link is lost for more than 10 seconds.
- Geofencing that prevents flight into restricted airspace.

## Battery and charging

The battery is a 30,000 mAh smart battery that can be swapped in about 90 seconds. With the Lumen Rapid Charger, a battery charges from 0% to 90% in 18 minutes. Each battery is rated for 600 charge cycles.

## Price and warranty

The Falcon X2 has a list price of $18,900, including two batteries and one Rapid Charger. The airframe and electronics are covered by a 2-year limited warranty. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 600 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Damage from crashes caused by operator error is not covered by the warranty.

## Differences from Falcon X1

Compared with the Falcon X1, Falcon X2 carries 2 kg more payload, flies 9 minutes longer when empty, and adds rear radar. The Falcon X1 reaches end of support in December 2027.
