# Customer Support FAQ


## When can I reach support?

Standard support is available Monday to Saturday, 06:00 to 20:00 in the customer's local time zone, by phone and chat. During peak spraying season (April to September) support is also open on Sundays from 08:00 to 14:00.

## What response time do Enterprise customers get?

Customers on the Enterprise plan get a guaranteed first response within 4 hours, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Standard-plan customers get a first response within one business day.

## Can I return a drone?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened products in like-new condition can be returned within 30 days with a 15% restocking fee. Products that have been flown cannot be returned, but they remain covered by the warranty.

## How do I send a drone for repair?

Open a return merchandise authorisation (RMA) request in the Lumen app. Lumen emails a prepaid shipping label. Repairs under warranty are usually completed within 5 to 7 business days after the drone arrives at the service centre. Enterprise customers can request a loaner drone while theirs is being repaired.

## How do firmware updates work?

Firmware updates are delivered through the Lumen app and installed while the drone is connected to its charger. Critical safety updates must be installed before the next flight; the drone will not arm its motors until a critical update is installed.

## How often should I calibrate the compass?

Calibrate the compass when you fly in a new location more than 50 km from the last calibration, after a firmware update, or whenever the app shows a compass warning.

## Do I need a licence to fly?

Licensing rules depend on the country. Lumen does not provide legal advice; customers are responsible for meeting their local aviation regulations.

## Can I buy spare parts?

Yes. Propellers, nozzles, batteries, and tank assemblies are sold in the Lumen online store. Spare batteries cost $1,150 each.
