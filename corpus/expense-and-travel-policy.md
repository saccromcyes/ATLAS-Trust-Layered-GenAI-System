# Expense and Travel Policy


## Submitting expenses

All business expenses are submitted through Ledgerly, the company's expense tool, within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses submitted after 30 days require written approval from the Finance Director. A receipt is required for every expense over $25. Any single expense over $500 needs approval from the employee's manager before it is incurred.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run once the expense report is approved.

## Flights

- Flights shorter than 6 hours: economy class.
- Flights of 6 hours or longer: premium economy class.
- Flights longer than 10 hours: business class may be booked, but only with prior approval from a Vice President.

Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Employees keep any airline loyalty points they earn.

## Hotels

The standard hotel limit is $220 per night, before taxes. In high-cost cities the limit is $300 per night. The high-cost cities are San Francisco, New York, London, Tokyo, and Zurich. Stays with friends or family are not reimbursed, but employees may claim the meal per diem.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are covered by a daily per diem instead of itemised receipts:

- Domestic travel: $65 per day.
- International travel: $90 per day.

On the first and last day of a trip, 75% of the per diem applies. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

## Ground transport and mileage

Taxis and ride-share are reimbursed for business travel. When an employee uses a personal car for business, mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Car rentals should be economy or compact class unless field equipment needs to be transported, in which case a pickup truck or van may be rented.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Corporate cards must never be used for personal expenses, even if the employee intends to repay the amount.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: traffic or parking fines, upgrades purchased for personal comfort, personal entertainment, and travel insurance (Lumen's corporate policy already covers business travel).
