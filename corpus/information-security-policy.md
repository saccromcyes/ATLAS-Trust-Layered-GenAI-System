# Information Security Policy


## 1. Accounts and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services. Employees must use the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on every company account. Employees with administrator privileges must use a hardware security key as their second factor; SMS codes are not accepted for administrators.

## 2. Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and must lock the screen after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating-system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile app.

## 3. Data classification

Lumen classifies information into four levels:

1. **Public** — information approved for release, such as the website and published product brochures.
2. **Internal** — day-to-day business information, such as meeting notes and internal announcements.
3. **Confidential** — information that would harm Lumen if leaked, such as financial plans, unreleased product designs, and source code.
4. **Restricted** — the most sensitive information, including customer farm-field maps, customer contracts, and employee personal data.

Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest, and must never be pasted into third-party AI tools or public chatbots. Confidential data may be used only with AI tools approved by the Security team.

## 4. Access control

Access follows the principle of least privilege. Managers review their team's access every quarter. When an employee leaves Lumen, all of their access must be revoked within 4 hours of their last working hour.

## 5. Reporting phishing and incidents

Suspected phishing emails must be reported within 1 hour using the "Report phishing" button in the mail client, or by posting in the #sec-report channel. Employees must not forward suspicious emails to colleagues. Lost devices and suspected security incidents follow the Incident Response Runbook.

## 6. Security training

Every employee completes security awareness training during their first week and again every year. Engineers also complete secure-coding training every year.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Information Security Officer and are valid for at most 6 months.
