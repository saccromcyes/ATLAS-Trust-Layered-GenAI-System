# Lumen Fieldworks Employee Handbook


## 1. Working hours and core hours

Lumen Fieldworks operates on a 40-hour working week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager. Meetings that include more than one time zone should be scheduled inside the overlapping core hours whenever possible.

## 2. Hybrid and remote work

Most roles are hybrid. Hybrid employees are expected in a Lumen office at least three days per week, and Tuesday is the company-wide in-office day. Fully remote arrangements are available only with written approval from a Vice President, and they are reviewed every 12 months. Employees working remotely must use a secured home network and follow the Information Security Policy.

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Longer periods abroad require approval from People Operations because they can create tax and immigration obligations.

## 3. Probation and notice periods

New employees complete a probation period of 90 days. During probation either party may end employment with one week of notice. After probation, the notice period is 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for people managers and directors.

## 4. Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Requests for PTO longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least three weeks in advance.

Lumen also observes 10 company holidays per year. When a company holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is a day off.

## 5. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than three consecutive working days. Sick days do not carry over.

## 6. Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave may be taken any time within the first 12 months after a birth, adoption, or foster placement, and may be split into at most two blocks.

## 7. Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Engineers may choose between a 14-inch and a 16-inch laptop. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## 8. Performance reviews

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Compensation changes are decided once a year as part of the October cycle.

## 9. Code of conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and farmers with respect. Concerns about misconduct can be raised with a manager, People Operations, or anonymously through the Ethics Line. Lumen does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
