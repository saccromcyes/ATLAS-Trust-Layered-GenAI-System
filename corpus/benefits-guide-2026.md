# 2026 Benefits Guide


## Health insurance

Lumen pays 100% of health insurance premiums for employees and 80% of premiums for dependents. Coverage starts on the employee's first day. Employees choose between two plans during onboarding: the Standard plan and the High-Deductible plan with a health savings account (HSA). Lumen contributes $1,000 per year to the HSA of employees on the High-Deductible plan.

## Retirement

Lumen matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a learning budget of $2,000 per calendar year for courses, books, certifications, and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Employees may also spend up to 5 working days per year on approved learning activities.

## Home office

Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of $600 and an internet allowance of $50 per month.

## Wellness

Each employee receives a wellness allowance of $75 per month, which can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, or meditation apps.

## Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers up to 8 free, confidential counselling sessions per issue per year for employees and their household members. The EAP also provides legal and financial consultations.

## Open enrollment

Open enrollment for 2027 benefits runs from 2 November to 20 November 2026. Changes take effect on 1 January 2027. Outside open enrollment, plan changes are allowed only after a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child, and must be requested within 30 days of the event.
